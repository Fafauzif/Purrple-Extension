Hello, little friend.

Purrple is ready to keep you company. Her tasks, timer and wardrobe work offline.

Load Purrple in Firefox

  1. Extract Purrple_Firefox_Extension.zip into a folder you will keep.
  2. In Firefox, enter about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in the address bar.
  3. Choose Load Temporary Add-on…, then select manifest.json in the extracted folder.
  4. Allow access to ordinary websites when Firefox asks, so Purrple can appear beside your work. You can change this later in Settings.
  5. Open Purrple from Firefox’s extensions menu. Pin her button to the toolbar if you like. The popup opens her dashboard or sidebar.
  6. Refresh website tabs that were already open. Purrple should appear near the bottom of supported pages.

This is an unsigned development build. Firefox removes a temporary add-on when the browser restarts. Load the same folder again to continue. Export a backup in Settings before uninstalling, clearing extension data, changing profiles or switching to a signed release.

A permanent installation in normal Firefox requires Mozilla signing. That final release step has been left for later, as agreed. Renaming this ZIP to XPI does not bypass signing.

A small daily routine

  • Add a task. Choose small, medium or large effort for 10, 20 or 30 coins.
  • Finish tasks to unlock permanent accessories in the wardrobe.
  • Start a focus session and let Purrple study alongside you. Finishing a session earns a small bonus.
  • Pick her up, pet her, play with yarn or send her home. Basic care is always free.
  • Hide her on a particular website or pause roaming whenever you want.

Keep your progress safe

Use Settings → Export backup regularly. Backups include tasks, purchases, outfits and preferences. A browser that is fully closed cannot deliver reminders until it is running again.

Firefox protects some pages, including its settings, add-ons pages and built-in PDF viewer. Use Purrple’s sidebar or dashboard there.

Requires Firefox 140 or later. All artwork, fonts and code are bundled. No AI model, login, subscription, analytics or remote service is used at runtime.
