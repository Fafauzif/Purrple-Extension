# Purrple 3.1

Today opens as a working sheet with task actions alongside the row and a Done imprint after saving a completion. The toolbar still opens directly to Today. On a website, click Purrple to open the action ring, then choose Tasks, Focus, Play, Talk, Care, or More. Agenda opens beside the webpage.

Purrple’s original sprite and equipment live in an illustrated study. The desk lamp reflects the running or paused timer, and the room and gift actions share the same setting. Use the appearance button to switch between Light and Night. In the room, open Arrange room when you want its placement controls.

Play lists the available games beside Purrple. Choosing one opens its own surface with the board and game controls. A new round begins when you press Begin. A round already underway resumes from its saved turn. Closing the window returns you to your activity.

The animation pass keeps the existing illustrated frames and re-registers their movement, with particular attention to turns, idle transitions, and the moment a click becomes a drag.

Design references: [Halftone Dossier and Pixel Study Room](https://github.com/Fafauzif/Design-with-Character/tree/40ba7e9) for the print sheet and stateful study, and [Monika After Story](https://github.com/Monika-After-Story/MonikaModDev/tree/01f1906) for choosing a game in the companion scene and playing it in its own surface. Purrple’s layouts, room art, and implementation are original.
