A little friend for your browser

Hello, I’m Purrple.

A tiny lavender cat for your plans, your study sessions, and the moments in between.

I love you

A little gift, made to keep you company.

Choose the archive for your browser. The Firefox ZIP supports temporary testing and the Brave and Chrome ZIP loads unpacked. A permanent Firefox installation uses the Mozilla-signed update.

Purrple’s local Calendar, focus timer, wardrobe and games work offline.

Five little steps.

1

Give Purrple a folder.

Extract the ZIP for your browser into a folder you’ll keep. Open the extracted folder and check that manifest.json is there. Keep the artwork and other files beside it.

If you receive a signed .xpi later, keep that file as it is. You don’t need to extract it.

2

Invite her into your browser.

FIREFOX TEMPORARY TEST

Enter about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in Firefox’s address bar. Choose Load Temporary Add-on… and select the extracted manifest.json.

Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it restarts. Load the same folder again to continue. Back up your progress regularly.

A SIGNED XPI, WHEN PROVIDED

Enter about:addons. Open the gear menu, choose Install Add-on From File…, select the signed .xpi, and follow Firefox’s installation prompts.

Use the signed update from the existing add-on entry to keep the same extension identity.

BRAVE OR CHROME

Open brave://extensions or chrome://extensions. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the folder with manifest.json.

3

Find her little button.

Open your browser’s Extensions menu, find Purrple, and pin her to the toolbar. The toolbar opens Today, with your task list and a place to add the next thing.

Allow website access where you’d like her to roam. Refresh website tabs that were already open before you installed her.

4

Say hello, then make one small plan.

Click Purrple on a webpage or in the toolbar popup. Choose Talk to say hello or Focus to start working. Capture a task in Today and set aside time for it in Calendar.

Drag to pick her up, release for a little tumble, or use her play controls. A gentle toss is fine. Her basic affection and play are always free.

5

Keep your little wins safe.

Open the full home’s Settings → Download backup and keep the JSON file somewhere safe. It includes local plans, progress and preferences. Do this before uninstalling, clearing extension data, changing browser profiles, or moving to a signed release.

To restore, choose your backup in Settings, check the confirmation, then restore it. An invalid backup leaves your existing progress alone.

One little task.

Small, medium and large tasks earn 1, 2 and 3 coins. Reopening the same task doesn’t earn a second reward.

A quiet study date.

Choose a focus session. Earn 1 coin for 15 minutes of focused work or 2 coins for 30 minutes or more. Shorter sessions still count in your focus history.

Something just for her.

Try on accessories before applying an outfit. Choose Play when you have a minute, or use Gifts and watch Purrple open your present.

If she needs a little help.

I can’t see Purrple on this page.

Check that roaming is on, the site is not hidden in Settings, and your browser has granted website access. Refresh the page. Use the toolbar or dashboard on protected browser pages.

She disappeared after I restarted Firefox.

This unsigned build is temporary. Return to about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox and load the same extracted manifest.json. A properly signed XPI is the permanent option once the owner has finished Mozilla signing.

I don’t want her on one website.

Use More → Hide on this site, or edit the hidden-host list in Settings. You can switch roaming off without losing tasks, coins or outfits.

When do reminders run?

Reminders run while your browser is open. When it opens again, Purrple catches up on overdue items and finished timers. Quiet hours and your computer’s notification settings can affect what you see.

How do I update her without losing progress?

Download a backup first. For an unpacked Brave or Chrome install, replace files in the same folder and reload from the extensions page. In Firefox, install the signed update for the same extension ID. Refresh your open webpages.
