A little friend for your Firefox

Hello, I’m Purrple.

A tiny lavender cat for your plans, your study sessions, and the moments in between.

I love you

A little gift, made to keep you company.

This copy is the unsigned development build. You can use it now with Firefox’s temporary setup. A normal permanent installation needs a Mozilla-signed .xpi file from the person who made Purrple.

All of Purrple’s artwork, fonts and features run locally. No AI service, subscription or internet connection is needed after installation.

Five little steps.

1

Give Purrple a folder.

Extract Purrple_Firefox_Extension.zip into a folder you’ll keep. Open the extracted folder and check that manifest.json is there. Keep the artwork and other files beside it.

If you receive a signed .xpi later, keep that file as it is. You don’t need to extract it.

2

Invite her into Firefox.

THIS DEVELOPMENT ZIP

Enter about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox in Firefox’s address bar. Choose Load Temporary Add-on… and select the extracted manifest.json.

Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it restarts. Load the same folder again to continue. Back up your progress regularly.

A SIGNED XPI, WHEN PROVIDED

Enter about:addons. Open the gear menu, choose Install Add-on From File…, select the signed .xpi, and follow Firefox’s installation prompts.

Renaming a ZIP to XPI does not sign it. Use this route only after Mozilla signing has actually been completed.

3

Find her little button.

Open Firefox’s Extensions button, find Purrple, and use her menu to Pin to Toolbar. Her toolbar popup keeps your tasks, focus timer, wardrobe and little conversations close by.

Allow website access where you’d like her to roam. Refresh website tabs that were already open before you installed her.

4

Say hello, then make one small plan.

Click the little control beside Purrple on a normal webpage, then choose Dashboard or Talk to open her compact panel. Add a task, start a study session, try on an accessory, or choose a conversation prompt. Her replies are lovingly written, fixed local lines.

Drag to pick her up, release for a little tumble, or use her play controls. A gentle toss is fine. Her basic affection and play are always free.

5

Keep your little wins safe.

Open the full home’s Settings → Export backup and keep the JSON file somewhere safe. It includes tasks, coins, outfits and preferences. Do this before uninstalling, clearing extension data, changing Firefox profiles, or moving to a signed release.

To restore, choose your backup in Settings, check the confirmation, then restore it. An invalid backup leaves your existing progress alone.

One little task.

Small, medium and large tasks earn 10, 20 and 30 coins. Reopening the same task doesn’t earn a second reward.

A quiet study date.

Choose a focus session. Purrple studies with you; finishing earns 5 coins. Pause whenever you need.

Something just for her.

Try on 15 accessories, save for a favorite, and keep it forever. Head and neck pieces can be worn together.

If she needs a little help.

I can’t see Purrple on this page.

Check that roaming is on, the site is not hidden in Settings, and Firefox has granted the website permission. Refresh the page. Firefox protects its settings, add-ons pages and built-in PDF viewer; use the toolbar popup, sidebar or full home on those pages.

She disappeared after I restarted Firefox.

This unsigned build is temporary. Return to about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox and load the same extracted manifest.json. A properly signed XPI is the permanent option once the owner has finished Mozilla signing.

I don’t want her on one website.

Use Purrple’s play menu to hide her on that website, or edit the hidden-host list in Settings. You can switch roaming off without losing tasks, coins or outfits.

Can she remind me while Firefox is closed?

Reminders run while Firefox is running. When it opens again, Purrple catches up on overdue items and finished timers. Quiet hours and your computer’s notification settings can affect what you see.

How do I update her without losing progress?

Export a backup first. Replace the files in the same extracted extension folder, then choose Reload for Purrple in about:debugging and refresh open webpage tabs. Keep the same Firefox profile and extension identity. Restore your backup if you need it.
